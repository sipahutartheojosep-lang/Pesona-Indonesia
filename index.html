About North Sumatra

North Sumatra is a province located on the island of Sumatra, Indonesia. This region is famous for its natural beauty, traditional culture, historical heritage, and delicious local food.

From the magnificent Lake Toba to the tropical forests of Bukit Lawang, North Sumatra offers different experiences for visitors.

Explore 4 Amazing Destinations

Discover four wonderful tourist destinations that represent the beauty of North Sumatra.

01. Lake Toba and Samosir Island

About the Destination

Lake Toba is a large volcanic lake located in North Sumatra. In the middle of the lake lies Samosir Island, which is well known for its beautiful landscapes and Batak culture.

Main Attractions

  • Beautiful Lake Toba scenery
  • Samosir Island
  • Traditional Batak houses
  • Tomok and Ambarita traditional villages

Activities

Visitors can enjoy the lake view, explore traditional villages, learn about Batak culture, and experience local traditions.

Location

Samosir Regency and the Lake Toba area, North Sumatra, Indonesia.

02. Bukit Lawang

About the Destination

Bukit Lawang is an ecotourism destination located near Gunung Leuser National Park. It is famous for its tropical rainforest and its role in the conservation of Sumatran orangutans.

Main Attractions

  • Tropical rainforest
  • Sumatran orangutans
  • Bahorok River
  • Beautiful natural environment

Activities

Visitors can enjoy nature, explore the surrounding area with official guides, and learn about wildlife conservation.

Location

Bahorok District, Langkat Regency, North Sumatra, Indonesia.

03. Berastagi

About the Destination

Berastagi is a highland town in Karo Regency. It is popular for its cool weather, beautiful mountain views, agricultural products, and relaxing atmosphere.

Main Attractions

  • Mount Sibayak
  • Gundaling Hill
  • Traditional fruit market
  • Beautiful highland scenery

Activities

Visitors can enjoy mountain views, visit local fruit markets, explore the highlands, and taste local products.

Location

Karo Regency, North Sumatra, Indonesia.

04. Sipiso-piso Waterfall

About the Destination

Sipiso-piso Waterfall is one of the most famous natural attractions in North Sumatra. The waterfall flows down from a high cliff and offers an impressive view of the Lake Toba area.

Main Attractions

  • High and beautiful waterfall
  • Lake Toba panorama
  • Green hills and natural scenery
  • Tongging village landscape

Activities

Visitors can enjoy the scenery, take photographs, and explore the surrounding Tongging area.

Location

Tongging Village, Karo Regency, North Sumatra, Indonesia.

Discover the Culture of North Sumatra

North Sumatra is also famous for its cultural diversity, especially Batak traditions. Traditional houses, music, dances, traditional clothing, and local food are important parts of its cultural identity.

Batak Traditional Culture
